----- Mega Man Randomizer -----
This app contains randomizers for multiple Mega Man games

- Supported games and versions -
• Rockman/Mega Man
	• Japanese Famicom version
	• North American NES version
	• Infidelity's Super Nintendo port
• Rockman 2/Mega Man 2
	• Japanese Famicom version
	• North American NES version
	• Infidelity's Super Nintendo port
• More coming!

- Important Info -
• The randomizer will output a spoiler txt and some IPS files, one for each supported version of a game
	• You will need a program like LunarIPS to apply these patches
• Passwords no longer work properly, use save states instead!

--------------------------------------------------

--- Rockman/Mega Man ---
- What can be randomized? -
• Shuffle boss weapons and magnet beam
• Shuffle/Randomize weaknesses of robot masters, and optionally castle bosses
	• Boss Sets (Shuffles weaknesses as groups, ie Elec Man could have Fire Man's weaknesses)
	• Per Boss (Shuffles each bosses weaknesses as separate pools, so something will always beat Cut Man in 2 hits)
	• Random Balanced (Creates new weaknesses that are weighted to be more similar to Mega Man 2's vanilla weakness distrubution)
	• Random Random (Creates new weaknesses that are totally random and have no rules at all)

- Boss changes -
• Elec Man deals half as much damage, but takes half as much damage from Rolling Cutter
• These boss weakness have been modified as such
	                         P    C    I    B    F    E    G
	Elec Man              |  1 |  5 |  0 |  2 |  1 |  1 |  4 |
	Yellow Devil (Wily 1) |  2 |  4 |  0 |  0 |  4 |  7 |  0 |
	Copy Robot (Wily 2)   |  2 |  2 |  0 |  7 |  4 |  4 |  0 |

- Equipment Changes -
• Magnet Beam has twice as much ammo

--- Rockman 2/Mega Man 2 ---
- What can be randomized? -
• Shuffle boss weapons and items 1, 2, & 3 (separately or together)
• Shuffle the stages on the stage select
• Shuffle/Randomize weaknesses of robot masters, and optionally castle bosses
	• Boss Sets (Shuffles weaknesses as groups, ie Quick Man could have Air Man's weaknesses)
	• Per Boss (Shuffles each bosses weaknesses as separate pools, so something will always beat Metal Man in 2 hits)
	• Random Balanced (Creates new weaknesses that are weighted to be more similar to Mega Man 2's vanilla weakness distrubution)
	• Random Random (Creates new weaknesses that are totally random and have no rules at all)
• Randomize how many bosses are affected by the time stopper and by how much
• Shuffle/Randomize the robot masters in each level and the rematches
	• Shuffle (each robot master appears exactly one time)
	• Random (each robot master could appear any number of times, even zero)
	• Single Random (one robot master will be the boss of all eight stages)
• Make 4 random robot masters and all wily castle bosses immune to the mega buster
	• this setting is experimental and may make the game less fun or potentially create impossible seeds, use at your own risk!

- Quality of Life changes -
• Energy bars fill faster
• Weapon get screen is faster
• Game over no longer resets E-Tanks
• The Crash Bomber walls in Boobeam Trap's room have been removed (Wily 4 boss)

- Boss changes -
• These boss weakness have been modified as such
	                         P    H    A    W    B    Q    C    M
	Boobeam Trap (Wily 4) |  2 | 20 |  0 | 10 |  0 |  2 | 20 |  0 |
	Wily Alien (Wily 6)   |  0 |  7 |  2 |  4 |  4 |  0 |  0 |  0 |

- Equipment Changes -
• Metal Blade/Quick Boomerang have half as much ammo
• Crash Bomber takes half the time to explode
• Item 1 has twice as much ammo
• Items 2 & 3 have 4 times as much ammo
• Atomic Fire's partially charged shots do half the damage of a fully charged shot, and uncharged shots do one quarter, to bosses specifically
